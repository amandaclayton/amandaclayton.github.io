Data and Replication Materials

Datasets and replication files from my published work.

QAROT: Quota Adoption and Reform over Time, 1947–2015

With Melanie M. Hughes, Pamela Paxton, and Pär Zetterberg. QAROT records the adoption, implementation, and reform of electoral gender quotas worldwide over nearly seven decades from the first historic quota adoption, capturing not just whether a country has a quota but how its design has changed over time. We are currently updating the dataset to extend through 2025.

Winner, 2022 APSA Lijphart/Przeworski/Verba Best Dataset Prize.

Download from openICPSR Accompanying article (PDF)

Citation: Hughes, Melanie M., Pamela Paxton, Amanda Clayton, and Pär Zetterberg. 2017. Quota Adoption and Reform over Time (QAROT), 1947–2015. Ann Arbor, MI: Inter-university Consortium for Political and Social Research. doi.org/10.3886/E100918V1

Replication files by article

Replication data and code for my journal articles are archived with the publishing journal. Most are available through the journal’s Dataverse or supplementary materials page:

  • American Political Science Review

    Facing Change (2023); Women Grab Back (2023); Gender and Party Discipline (2021); Electoral Gender Quotas and Democratic Legitimacy (2025). Archived at the APSR Dataverse.

  • American Journal of Political Science

    Representation Increases Women’s Influence in Climate Deliberations (2025) — replication materials, computational reproducibility verified by the Cornell Center for Social Sciences. All Male Panels? (2019) is archived at the AJPS Dataverse.

  • Journal of Politics

    Quota Shocks (2018); When the Personal Becomes Political (2026). Archived at the JOP Dataverse.

  • Other journals

    Replication materials for articles in Comparative Political Studies, Politics & Gender, PSRM, JEPS, BJPS, and World Development are available through each journal’s supplementary materials.
